Annotated View
Model
Fine-tuned MatSciBERT — a domain-specific BERT pre-trained on materials science literature. Adapted for token classification with BIO tagging over 4 entity types.
Training
Trained on 40 annotated papers from materials science journals. Few-shot strategy used to maximise performance with limited labelled data.
Architecture
AutoModelForTokenClassification with a linear classification head. BIO tagging scheme. Max sequence length 512 tokens. 4 entity classes → 12 label IDs.
